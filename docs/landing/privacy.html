🌸 Desktop Companion

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 29, 2026

In short: Desktop Companion has no account, no analytics and no telemetry, and we run no server that receives your data. Everything is stored on your own computer. The app only goes online for features you turn on, and it talks directly to the service that feature needs.

Who we are

Desktop Companion is a free, open-source desktop app made by TechDecoded. The source code is public at github.com/xShiroeNguyenx/desktop-companion, so anything described here can be checked against the code.

Data stored on your computer

The app keeps its data as plain files in its own config folder on your computer. This includes:

  • Settings, including the AI API key you enter. The key is kept in the local settings file and is only sent to the AI provider you picked.
  • Chat history with your companion.
  • Your content: tasks, notes, reminders, bookmarks, the websites you ask it to monitor, and the city you choose for weather.
  • Progress: candy, collection, unlocked characters and game achievements.

None of this is uploaded to us. You can read, back up or delete these files at any time, and uninstalling the app together with its config folder removes them.

What the app reads on your computer

  • Selected text. When you select text or press the capture shortcut, the app copies that text (restoring your clipboard afterwards) so it can translate it or draft a reply. It does not record keystrokes.
  • Activity state. To avoid interrupting you, it checks how long the mouse and keyboard have been idle and whether a full-screen app is running or the screen is locked. It does not read window titles, process names or screen contents for this.
  • System stats. The optional system monitor reads CPU, memory, battery and the names of the busiest processes to show them in the widget. These stay on your computer.

Data sent over the network

Each connection below happens only when you use the feature, and goes straight from your computer to the service listed. Their own privacy policies apply to what they receive.

FeatureSent toWhat is sent
AI chat, translation, quick reply Anthropic (api.anthropic.com) or Google Gemini (generativelanguage.googleapis.com), whichever you choose Your API key, your message or selected text, recent conversation context and the companion's persona instructions
Weather Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com) The name or coordinates of the city you picked. No IP or GPS location lookup
Site monitor The websites you add A plain request to check that the site is up. No cookies or personal data
Updates and character downloads GitHub (github.com) Standard download requests for new versions and character packs
Quick launcher and bookmarks Your web browser Opens the search or link you typed. The app itself sends nothing

Like any internet request, these services can see your IP address. We do not receive any of this traffic.

What we do not do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No analytics, tracking, crash reporting or advertising.
  • No selling or sharing of data, since we never receive it.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them. Using the AI features is also subject to the age requirements of the AI provider you choose.

Changes to this policy

If a new feature changes what data leaves your computer, we will update this page and its effective date before that version is released. Past versions are kept in the project's public history on GitHub.

Contact

Questions or concerns about privacy: open an issue at github.com/xShiroeNguyenx/desktop-companion/issues.
